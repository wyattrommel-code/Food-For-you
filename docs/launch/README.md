# Mealsolved launch kit

Prepared September 25, 2026. Use after Android 1.0.9 is available to internal testers. Social publishing is intentionally pending account access and real footage.

## Start here

1. Verify the tester release in Play Console and on one real Android phone.
2. Record the four Week 1 videos in [post-scripts.md](post-scripts.md). Scripts are ready to read; do not invent testimonials or filmed results.
3. Complete the website signup connection described in [signup-and-operations.md](signup-and-operations.md) before using a signup call to action.
4. Publish four original videos weekly, adapting the same clean export for TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels. Reuse the best two on YouTube Shorts.
5. Use [calendar.md](calendar.md) for the 30-day schedule and [results-template.md](results-template.md) for weekly review.

## Positioning

**Less deciding. More eating.** Mealsolved helps answer “What should I eat?” with three choices, filters, and the ability to keep one choice while refreshing the others. Familiar home meals are the subject. Saving money is a possible side effect, not a promised result.

Primary audiences: tired shift workers, busy households, and young adults learning to feed themselves. Speak to their situation rather than labeling them as bad cooks or bad parents.

Profile bio:

> Stuck on what to eat? Three ideas. Keep a favorite. Refresh the rest. Join our Android test ↓

Pin V01 (problem and demo), V02 (keep one/refresh), and V04 (founder/test invitation). Use the existing Mealsolved logo, warm white backgrounds, dark text, and app red. Use a matching handle where available; verify availability rather than asserting ownership.

## Production recipe

- Four original 20–35-second videos weekly. Vertical 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Use a phone, daylight, existing editor, and your own voice; no subscription required.
- Hook in the first two seconds. Show the app by second eight. Finish with one action.
- Burn in readable captions, at most two lines at once. Keep critical text away from the bottom and right-side platform controls. Check each platform's preview before posting.
- Record a clean master without another platform's watermark. Use original audio or music licensed for the account and destination; platform music rights may not transfer.
- Record the actual production build with demo data you control. Hide emails, notifications, private household names, and personal schedules.
- Use real cooking footage for “I made this” posts. AI catalog pictures may illustrate the app, but must not be passed off as a meal you cooked.
- Do not claim current community ratings/comments, an iOS release date, a recipe count, or allergy safety without verification. Never invent a customer quote or statistics.
- TikTok: conversational hook and direct demonstration. Instagram: clean cover and save/share intent. Facebook: one sentence of household context. YouTube: descriptive title, e.g. “Keep one dinner idea and refresh the others | Mealsolved”.

Suggested neutral tags when relevant: #DinnerIdeas #EasyMeals #MealPlanning #Mealsolved. Tags are context, not a growth guarantee.

## Weekly time budget

75 minutes filming; 45 minutes app/food footage; 45 minutes editing and captions; ten minutes daily responding; twenty minutes reviewing results. Batch recording once a week is the default. Do not create four separate productions for four platforms.

## Evidence behind the approach

Early hooks, vertical video and clear demonstrations follow [TikTok's creative guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads?redirected=1). Its advertising findings inform our experiments; they do not predict organic virality.

Internal testing requires tester access and an opt-in link: [Google Play testing guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334?hl=en-GB). Public production eligibility is a separate check, including closed-testing requirements where applicable: [Google Play requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en).
