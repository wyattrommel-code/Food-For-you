# Signup and publishing operations

## Access and launch gates

- Social publishing waits for Wyatt to connect accounts. Do not request passwords in chat. Confirm account handles, business type, available free scheduling tools, and profile links when access arrives.
- mealsolved.app currently uses the Mealsolvedwebsite repository and a Mailchimp embedded signup form. Existing copy contains outdated launch dates and removed features; corrected website work belongs in that repository.
- Before advertising signup, confirm the form submits to the intended audience and stores source/consent fields correctly. A form that merely looks successful is not sufficient.
- Tester email is needed for the Play access list. Explain manual invitations and that signup is not immediate app access. Do not promise a response time until someone can maintain it.
- Keep tester access messages separate from optional marketing enrollment. Do not silently subscribe all testers to a newsletter. Source and feedback are optional. Do not collect phone numbers or household details.
- Do not send invitations, followups or social replies from this kit automatically. This task creates drafts; social access and actual recipients come later.

## Approved tester flow

1. Interest form: email required; optional source (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, Friend, Other); optional short feedback; separate unchecked marketing option if supported by verified provider fields.
2. Confirmation: “Thanks for your interest. We're inviting Android testers manually. Signing up doesn't grant immediate access. Watch for an invitation with the next steps.” Do not show this before the provider confirms receipt.
3. Owner adds the exact Google account email to the existing internal tester list and sends the opt-in link privately.
4. Tester opens link with that account, joins, installs or updates, and tries one meal decision.
5. Ask for a short experience report after use; track installation and a second-week return with their consent.

Existing opt-in link, for invited testers only: https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4701571696443292910

## Message drafts

**Invitation (send only after access is granted):**

> You're invited to test Mealsolved on Android. Open https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4701571696443292910 using the Google account you gave us, join the test, then follow the Google Play install link. Try choosing one meal, holding a choice while refreshing, and adding a meal to the planner. If something doesn't work, reply with what happened—no password needed.

**Feedback request:**

> Did Mealsolved help you decide what to eat? What did you pick, and what slowed you down? One sentence is enough. If you send a screenshot, please hide personal information.

**iPhone inquiry:**

> We're testing on Android first. We don't have a confirmed iPhone release date to share yet.

**Bug comment:**

> Thanks for telling us. Which step failed, and which app version are you using? Please don't post your email or account details here.

**Community recipes inquiry:**

> Community recipes are something we want to explore. They aren't part of the current release. What would you want to share?

## Release and campaign gates

Internal tester release can proceed after build and regression checks. Broader recruitment should wait until signup email delivery, recipe loading, images, and planner work reliably on actual Android devices. Public Play release requires its own production-access check. No advertising spend or subscription renewal is part of this launch kit.
