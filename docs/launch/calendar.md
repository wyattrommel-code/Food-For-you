## Four carousel briefs

Make five 1080 × 1350 slides each. Large title, one point per slide, actual food photo when available. Ingredient suggestions below are editorial topics; confirm a matching live recipe and its times before using app screenshots.

**C01 — Too tired to decide dinner?** Slide 1 title. Slide 2 sandwich and chips. Slide 3 butter noodles with a simple side. Slide 4 breakfast for dinner. Slide 5 “Want help choosing? Mealsolved · Android testing · Link in profile.” Caption: “Familiar food counts. Save these for a low-energy evening.”

**C02 — Familiar ingredients, five possible meals.** Slide 1 title. Slide 2 tacos or burritos. Slide 3 easy pasta or noodles. Slide 4 eggs and toast. Slide 5 testing invitation. Caption: “You don't need an unfamiliar shopping list to start cooking at home.”

**C03 — A three-day meal plan.** Slide 1 title. Slide 2 Monday sandwich meal. Slide 3 Tuesday tacos. Slide 4 Wednesday breakfast for dinner. Slide 5 “Choose your own three in Mealsolved.” Caption: “Swap the days. Change the meals. A plan is a starting point.”

**C04 — What should we add next?** Slide 1 title. Slides 2–4 actual top requested meal categories; if no data, present sandwiches, oven meals, and desserts as choices, not alleged popular requests. Slide 5 “Tell us what you'd actually cook.” Caption: “Help shape a recipe library you'll use.”
